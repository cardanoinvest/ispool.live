QUESTIONS

Know before you delegate.

info@ispool.live
Does this website take custody of my ADA?+

No. This website is informational and does not connect to your wallet. Review and authorize any delegation only inside your own wallet.

Are rewards guaranteed?+

No. Rewards vary with network conditions, pool performance, and other factors. The site deliberately does not display a projected return.

Why are some statistics unavailable?+

The API can be unavailable, rate-limited, or restricted. Cached data is labelled with its fetch time. Use the explorer to verify independently.

Will delegating here give me NIGHT tokens?+

ADA delegation alone does not guarantee NIGHT tokens or any additional distribution. Eligibility depends on official program rules.
